# Post hole drilling

A fence needs dozens of identical holes. A mini excavator auger drills each one in minutes, all to the same depth.

## What's included

- Drilling holes for posts
- Same depth and diameter every time
- Holes for planting trees and vines
- Gathering the dug soil

## When you need it

- Building a fence
- Shed or awning posts
- Lighting or sign posts
- Planting an orchard or vineyard

## Why an auger

- An auger drills a neat round hole without loosening the soil around it, so the post stands firmer.

## Many holes, fast

- Fences and vineyards need lots of holes — with an auger that goes much faster.

## Frequently asked questions

### How deep can you drill?

We choose depth and diameter based on the post and the soil.

### Can you drill in rocky ground?

In very rocky ground we sometimes need the breaker too — we'll assess on site.

### How much does a mini excavator cost?

It depends on the size and length of the job, the soil, site access, the attachment needed (bucket, hydraulic breaker, auger) and whether soil or debris has to be removed. Call us or send a photo on WhatsApp — we agree the price in advance.

### Do you hire it out with an operator?

Yes — the mini excavator always comes with our experienced operator. You just tell us what needs doing.
