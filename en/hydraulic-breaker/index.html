Concrete breaking with a hydraulic breaker

Old concrete floors, foundations, asphalt or rocky ground take weeks by hand. A hydraulic breaker on a mini excavator breaks them quickly.

Call 514 90 80 90WhatsApp

What's included

  • Breaking concrete slabs and floors
  • Breaking up old foundations
  • Breaking asphalt and rocky ground
  • Loading and removing rubble

When you need it

  • Removing old concrete in a yard
  • Demolishing a building or fence foundation
  • Digging a pit in rocky ground
  • Clearing a site before renovation

What a hydraulic breaker is

A hydraulic breaker is a hammer mounted on the excavator boom that breaks concrete and rock with repeated blows.

Rubble removal

The excavator loads the broken concrete straight onto the truck — the site stays clean.

Frequently asked questions

What can a breaker break?+

Concrete, reinforced concrete slabs, asphalt and rocky ground.

Will you remove the rubble?+

Yes — we load it and take it away with our own truck.

How much does a mini excavator cost?+

It depends on the size and length of the job, the soil, site access, the attachment needed (bucket, hydraulic breaker, auger) and whether soil or debris has to be removed. Call us or send a photo on WhatsApp — we agree the price in advance.

Do you hire it out with an operator?+

Yes — the mini excavator always comes with our experienced operator. You just tell us what needs doing.
