# What can a mini excavator do?

A mini excavator is small but versatile: with different buckets, a hydraulic breaker and an auger it handles dozens of jobs — even in narrow yards where big machines can't go. Here are the 12 most common ones.

## 12 jobs a mini excavator does

- [Earthworks with a mini excavator](https://eqskavatori.ge/en/earthworks/) — Digging, pits, levelling and soil removal.
- [Trench digging](https://eqskavatori.ge/en/trench-digging/) — Trenches for water, sewer and cable lines.
- [Foundation excavation](https://eqskavatori.ge/en/foundation-excavation/) — Foundations for houses, fences and outbuildings.
- [Septic tank installation](https://eqskavatori.ge/en/septic-tank-installation/) — Pit digging, tank setting and connection.
- [Sewer line installation](https://eqskavatori.ge/en/sewer-line-installation/) — Trench, pipes and connection — in one go.
- [Water line installation](https://eqskavatori.ge/en/water-line-installation/) — Trench, pipe laying and backfilling.
- [Yard and site levelling](https://eqskavatori.ge/en/site-levelling/) — Levelling, grading and surplus soil removal.
- [Concrete breaking with a hydraulic breaker](https://eqskavatori.ge/en/hydraulic-breaker/) — Concrete, asphalt and rock — with a hydraulic breaker.
- [Post hole drilling](https://eqskavatori.ge/en/post-hole-drilling/) — Holes for posts, fences and trees.
- [Construction debris loading and removal](https://eqskavatori.ge/en/debris-removal/) — Rubble, soil and waste — loaded and hauled away.
- [Demolition with a mini excavator](https://eqskavatori.ge/en/demolition/) — Small structures, fences and concrete taken down.
- [Garden and landscaping work](https://eqskavatori.ge/en/landscaping/) — Stump removal, planting holes, terraces and shaping.

## Mini excavator, Bobcat or a big excavator?

- A Bobcat (compact loader) is good for moving soil and debris, but for deep digging a mini excavator is better. A big excavator is used for large pits and big sites, but it can't get into narrow yards and damages surfaces more. For private houses and small sites, a mini excavator is the most practical choice.

## When to choose a mini excavator

- Access to the yard or plot is narrow
- Work is close to a building, fence or trees
- You need precise digging — a trench, foundation or pit
- You want minimal damage to the yard surface

## Frequently asked questions

### What is a mini excavator used for?

Digging, excavating trenches, foundations and septic pits, laying pipes, breaking concrete with a hydraulic breaker, drilling holes, demolition and levelling.

### Will a mini excavator fit through a narrow gate?

Usually yes — mini excavators are built for tight spaces. We'll give you the exact dimensions when you call.

### Is a mini excavator the same as a Bobcat?

People often use the name, but a Bobcat is a compact loader, while a mini excavator is built for digging pits, trenches and foundations. If you need digging, a mini excavator is what you want.
