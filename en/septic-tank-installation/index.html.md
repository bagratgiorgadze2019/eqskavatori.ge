# Septic tank installation

Where there's no sewer network, a septic tank is the best solution. We dig the pit with a mini excavator, set the tank and connect it to the house.

## What's included

- Digging the septic pit
- Setting the septic tank
- Laying and connecting the pipe from the house
- Backfilling and removing surplus soil

## When you need it

- A country or village house
- A new house with no sewer network
- Replacing an old septic tank
- Upsizing a septic tank

## Choosing the spot

- A septic tank must sit far enough from the house, a well and the fence, and be reachable for an emptying truck. We choose the spot together.

## Pit size

- The pit size depends on the tank volume — tell us the size in advance or send us the tank details.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do you supply the septic tank?

Usually the customer buys the tank — we dig, set and connect it, and can help you choose.

### How much does a mini excavator cost?

It depends on the size and length of the job, the soil, site access, the attachment needed (bucket, hydraulic breaker, auger) and whether soil or debris has to be removed. Call us or send a photo on WhatsApp — we agree the price in advance.

### Do you hire it out with an operator?

Yes — the mini excavator always comes with our experienced operator. You just tell us what needs doing.
