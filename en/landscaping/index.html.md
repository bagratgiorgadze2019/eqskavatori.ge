# Garden and landscaping work

Creating a new garden or reshaping an old one takes a lot of earthmoving. With a mini excavator we pull out old stumps, dig planting holes and shape the ground the way you want.

## What's included

- Removing tree and shrub stumps
- Holes for planting trees
- Building terraces and slopes
- Digging a pool or pond

## When you need it

- Planting a new garden or vineyard
- Getting rid of old stumps
- Terracing a slope
- A pool or decorative pond in the yard

## Stump removal

- A stump left after felling is hard to dig out by hand — the excavator pulls it out roots and all.

## Slopes and terraces

- We terrace slopes so the soil doesn't wash away and the garden is easier to look after.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can you remove a tree stump?

Yes — we pull out the stump with its roots and fill the hole.

### Can you dig a pool?

Yes — we dig it to size and take the soil away.

### How much does a mini excavator cost?

It depends on the size and length of the job, the soil, site access, the attachment needed (bucket, hydraulic breaker, auger) and whether soil or debris has to be removed. Call us or send a photo on WhatsApp — we agree the price in advance.

### Do you hire it out with an operator?

Yes — the mini excavator always comes with our experienced operator. You just tell us what needs doing.
