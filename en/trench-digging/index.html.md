# Trench digging

To lay a water pipe, sewer line or power cable you need a narrow, accurate trench. A mini excavator digs it quickly without wrecking your yard.

## What's included

- Trench digging to the required depth and width
- Backfilling after the pipe or cable is laid
- Removal of surplus soil
- Pipe laying — on request

## When you need it

- Laying a water pipe
- Sewer line from the house to the septic tank
- Power cable in the yard
- Drainage trench

## Depth and slope

- A sewer trench needs the right slope and a water pipe enough depth so it doesn't freeze in winter. We measure this on site.

## One team for the whole job

- We don't just dig — we can also lay the pipe and backfill the trench.

## Frequently asked questions

### How deep do you dig trenches?

It depends on the purpose — water, sewer or cable. We agree depth and width on site.

### Do you lay the pipes too?

Yes — we lay sewer and water pipes and backfill the trench.

### How much does a mini excavator cost?

It depends on the size and length of the job, the soil, site access, the attachment needed (bucket, hydraulic breaker, auger) and whether soil or debris has to be removed. Call us or send a photo on WhatsApp — we agree the price in advance.

### Do you hire it out with an operator?

Yes — the mini excavator always comes with our experienced operator. You just tell us what needs doing.
