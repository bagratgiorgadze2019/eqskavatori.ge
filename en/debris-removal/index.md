# Construction debris loading and removal

After renovation or demolition, piles of rubble, old concrete and soil are left behind. We load them with a mini excavator and haul them away in our own truck.

## What's included

- Loading rubble with a mini excavator
- Removing dug soil
- Removing old concrete and bricks
- Cleaning up the site

## When you need it

- After renovation or demolition
- After digging a foundation or pit
- Clearing a yard
- After pulling down an old structure

## Machine loading

- Loading by hand takes hours — the excavator loads debris straight into the truck.

## All in one go

- If we also do the earthworks, removal is planned for the same day — no separate haulage company needed.

## Frequently asked questions

### What does the removal price depend on?

The volume, the distance and whether excavator loading is needed — call us or send a photo.

### Can I order removal only?

Yes — if the waste is already piled up, we come, load it and take it away.

### How much does a mini excavator cost?

It depends on the size and length of the job, the soil, site access, the attachment needed (bucket, hydraulic breaker, auger) and whether soil or debris has to be removed. Call us or send a photo on WhatsApp — we agree the price in advance.

### Do you hire it out with an operator?

Yes — the mini excavator always comes with our experienced operator. You just tell us what needs doing.
