# Foundation excavation

A foundation needs exact dimensions and a flat bottom. A mini excavator digs strip foundations and pits to size — even on narrow plots.

## What's included

- Strip foundation trenches
- Foundation pit excavation
- Levelling the bottom
- Removal of dug soil

## When you need it

- Building a private house
- A fence or retaining wall
- A shed, garage or outbuilding
- An extension to an existing house

## Dimensions from the plan

- We dig to the markings — width and depth should match the project or your builder's instructions.

## Narrow plots

- A mini excavator works safely close to a neighbour's fence or building.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do you dig to the given dimensions?

Yes — following the markings and the plan, to the required width and depth.

### Will you take the soil away?

Yes — we load and remove the soil, or leave it on site.

### How much does a mini excavator cost?

It depends on the size and length of the job, the soil, site access, the attachment needed (bucket, hydraulic breaker, auger) and whether soil or debris has to be removed. Call us or send a photo on WhatsApp — we agree the price in advance.

### Do you hire it out with an operator?

Yes — the mini excavator always comes with our experienced operator. You just tell us what needs doing.
