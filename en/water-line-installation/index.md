# Water line installation

To stop a water pipe freezing in winter, it has to be laid deep enough. We dig the trench with a mini excavator, lay the pipe and backfill.

## What's included

- Trench to the required depth
- Laying the water pipe
- Sand bedding to protect the pipe
- Backfilling and levelling the surface

## When you need it

- Water supply for a new house
- A line from a well or tank to the house
- Garden irrigation
- Replacing an old pipe

## Depth and protection

- We lay the pipe deep enough not to freeze and bed it in sand above and below so stones can't damage it.

## Fast and tidy

- A mini excavator digs a narrow trench, so your yard and lawn are barely disturbed.

## Frequently asked questions

### How deep will you lay the pipe?

Below the depth where it could freeze — we choose the exact depth on site.

### Do you supply the pipe?

Usually the customer buys the pipe — we'll help you choose.

### How much does a mini excavator cost?

It depends on the size and length of the job, the soil, site access, the attachment needed (bucket, hydraulic breaker, auger) and whether soil or debris has to be removed. Call us or send a photo on WhatsApp — we agree the price in advance.

### Do you hire it out with an operator?

Yes — the mini excavator always comes with our experienced operator. You just tell us what needs doing.
