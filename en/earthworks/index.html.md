# Earthworks with a mini excavator

Digging by hand takes days — a mini excavator does the same job in hours, and it fits into narrow yards.

## What's included

- Digging and pit excavation
- Moving and backfilling soil
- Loading and removing soil
- Planning the job on site

## When you need it

- Starting a house or outbuilding
- Changing the level of a yard or garden
- Digging a basement or a pit
- Replacing old soil

## Why a mini excavator

- Its small size lets it pass narrow gates and work close to buildings, fences and trees — where a big excavator can't go.

## Soil and removal

- We leave the dug soil on site for backfilling, or load it and take it away with our own truck.

## Frequently asked questions

### How long does digging take?

It depends on the volume and the soil. A pit or trench usually takes a few hours — we'll give you the exact time after seeing the site.

### Do you work in rocky ground?

Yes — we break hard ground and rock with a hydraulic breaker.

### How much does a mini excavator cost?

It depends on the size and length of the job, the soil, site access, the attachment needed (bucket, hydraulic breaker, auger) and whether soil or debris has to be removed. Call us or send a photo on WhatsApp — we agree the price in advance.

### Do you hire it out with an operator?

Yes — the mini excavator always comes with our experienced operator. You just tell us what needs doing.
