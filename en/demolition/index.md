# Demolition with a mini excavator

Is an old shed, garage, fence or wall in the way of new construction? A mini excavator takes small structures down safely, and we load the debris on the spot.

## What's included

- Taking down small structures and sheds
- Removing fences and walls
- Breaking concrete structures with a breaker
- Loading and removing debris

## When you need it

- New construction in place of an old structure
- Enlarging a yard
- Replacing an old fence
- Removing a damaged structure

## For small structures

- A mini excavator is made for small and medium structures; a large building needs other machinery.

## Precise and safe

- The machine works close to neighbouring buildings and fences without damaging them.

## Frequently asked questions

### What do you demolish?

Sheds, garages, fences, walls and small concrete structures.

### Will you remove the debris?

Yes — we load it and take it away with our own truck.

### How much does a mini excavator cost?

It depends on the size and length of the job, the soil, site access, the attachment needed (bucket, hydraulic breaker, auger) and whether soil or debris has to be removed. Call us or send a photo on WhatsApp — we agree the price in advance.

### Do you hire it out with an operator?

Yes — the mini excavator always comes with our experienced operator. You just tell us what needs doing.
