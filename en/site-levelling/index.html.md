# Yard and site levelling

You can't lay a lawn or a path on uneven ground. We level the plot with a mini excavator and grade it so rainwater runs away from the house.

## What's included

- Spreading and levelling soil
- Grading for water run-off
- Loading and removing surplus soil
- Preparing ground for lawns and paths

## When you need it

- Setting up a new yard
- Before sowing a lawn
- Preparing parking or a path
- Clearing up after construction

## Slope and water

- On a well-graded plot, rainwater flows away from the house instead of pooling at the foundation.

## After construction

- After building work we clear leftover soil and debris and level the site.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can you prepare ground for a lawn?

Yes — we level the plot and give it the right slope.

### Will you take surplus soil away?

Yes — we load it and remove it with our own truck.

### How much does a mini excavator cost?

It depends on the size and length of the job, the soil, site access, the attachment needed (bucket, hydraulic breaker, auger) and whether soil or debris has to be removed. Call us or send a photo on WhatsApp — we agree the price in advance.

### Do you hire it out with an operator?

Yes — the mini excavator always comes with our experienced operator. You just tell us what needs doing.
